This Privacy Policy explains how Shanghai Quanjie Technology Co., Ltd. collects, uses, shares, and protects personal data in connection with the **WordTT** mini game and related services (the "**Services**"). WordTT is accessed through TikTok; your use of TikTok is also governed by TikTok's own privacy policy, which is separate from this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Services.

## 1. Personal Data We Collect

- **Gameplay data:** your daily-challenge results, guesses, completion time, number of rows used, boosters used, and local game progress.
- **Social data (only with your authorization):** if you use the friend leaderboard or sharing, TikTok may provide us with your nickname (display name), avatar, an in-game open ID, and your mutual-friend relationship data.
- **Device and technical data:** approximate device and system information (such as device model, operating system, and language) and diagnostic/log information.
- **Advertising data:** if you watch a rewarded advertisement, the ad provider may process an advertising identifier and limited device information.

We do **not** collect your real name, phone number, government ID, precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone, and we do **not** ask for payment information (WordTT does not sell items for real money).

## 2. How We Use Personal Data

We use personal data to: provide the daily challenge and save/restore your progress; run boosters; display the friend leaderboard and enable sharing (when authorized); serve and measure rewarded advertisements; prevent cheating, fraud, and security incidents; diagnose errors and improve the Services; and comply with applicable law and platform requirements.

## 3. Legal Bases for Processing

Where required by applicable law, we process personal data on the following bases: your **consent** (for example, social features and certain advertising); the **performance of a contract** with you (to provide the game you request); our **legitimate interests** (such as security, fraud prevention, and improving the Services), balanced against your rights; and **compliance with legal obligations**.

## 4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The game stores small amounts of data **locally on your device** (through the platform's local storage) to save your daily progress, settings, and boosters. This local data stays on your device and can be cleared through TikTok. Advertising and platform SDKs may use identifiers as described above. We do not use these technologies for purposes other than those described here.

## 5. How We Share Personal Data

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

- **TikTok / ByteDance (platform provider):** cloud storage of your leaderboard score, login/authorization, sharing, and advertising are provided through the TikTok Mini Games platform, subject to TikTok's terms and privacy policy.
- **Advertising and analytics partners:** to serve and measure rewarded ads and understand aggregate usage.
- **Service providers:** vendors providing hosting, storage, and technical infrastructure on our behalf.
- **Legal and safety:** where required by law, legal process, or a lawful government request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, the public, or us.
- **Corporate transactions:** in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

## 6. International Transfers

Because we operate from **Shanghai, People's Republic of China**, we may process and store personal data in **China** and in other countries where we or our service providers operate. Where required by the laws of your place of residence, we use appropriate safeguards (such as contractual protections or your consent) for such transfers. By using the Services, you understand that your data may be processed outside your country.

## 7. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law, after which we delete or anonymize it.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Your Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable law — including Japan's **APPI**, Indonesia's **Personal Data Protection Law**, the Philippines' **Data Privacy Act of 2012**, Malaysia's **PDPA 2010**, and, where applicable, the EU/UK **GDPR** — you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, to object to certain processing, to withdraw consent, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below. You can also clear the game's local data and manage social/authorization permissions within TikTok.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13 or below the minimum age required in your jurisdiction (as applied, for example, through TikTok's age-gating). We do not knowingly collect personal data from such children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.
